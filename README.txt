=============================

- script.js                   Season transitions, mobile menu and enquiry form

PREVIEW ON YOUR COMPUTER
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Keep index.html, styles.css, script.js and the assets folder together.
3. Double-click index.html to open the site in a browser.

PUBLISHING ON HOSTING
Upload the contents of this folder (not the ZIP itself) into your domain's document-root
folder, commonly public_html. Keep index.html directly inside that folder and keep the
assets folder alongside it.

SEASON EXPERIENCE
The main hero automatically cycles through Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter. Visitors can
also choose any season using the season buttons. Each season has its own garden image, colour
mood and floating particle animation. Reduced-motion accessibility preferences are respected.

CONTACT FORM
The enquiry form opens the visitor's email application with the message addressed to
info@sirgaedens.com. It does not send or store enquiries on a server by itself. For direct
website submissions, connect a form service or server-side endpoint.
